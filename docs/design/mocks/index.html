Mandate · the mockup set

Eleven pages for three audiences. Each answers one question first, explains it on one click, and shows the machinery only when asked. Throwaway HTML for direction, not product code.

Light pages are for working and reading (the chef's pages, the request walkthrough). Dark pages are for watching and presenting (Valio's room, the story, the architecture).
One scenario everywhere: Hotel Rautatientori, Mon 7 Dec 22:11, cut-off Tue 15:00, delivery Thu 10. The ham shortage has the same numbers on every page. Click a product name to open its sheet.
