One gate, every door

Your kitchen, your Claude and Valio's ops all pass the same gate.

Every change goes through one function. Approval is a hash of the exact terms, a retry returns the same receipt, and an outside agent can plan but never approve.

Honest about supply

“Planned” is not “covered”. We draw the difference.

Supplier confirmedbacked by a receipt
Ordered, awaiting suppliercommitted, not yet confirmed
Planned, known stockValio states a quantity; not reserved
Planned, unknown stocknever counted as cover
Not coveredsaid out loud, never hidden

Solid means observed, hatched means not yet true, outline means missing. It reads in greyscale.
