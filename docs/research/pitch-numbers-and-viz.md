**Academic anchors** (all V, from abstract or record pages):
- **Human override of ordering tools.** Store managers systematically shift automated order advice, for reasons the system can't see, such as handling cost and case-pack size relative to demand. The study covered 19,417 item-store pairs ([van Donselaar et al., *Mgmt Sci* 56(5), 2010](https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v56y2010i5p766-784.html)). In a 12-month randomised field experiment on 30k SKUs, buyers' overrides cut profit by **5.77%** overall but raised it by **>23% for growth-stage products**. The humans knew something the data didn't ([Kesavan & Kushwaha, *Mgmt Sci* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3743)). Design implication: ask the human at the exceptions, not on every line.
- **Forecasting covers.** Random forests forecast Swedish school-canteen attendance better than a simple neural net and saved money ([Malefors et al., *Socio-Econ. Plan. Sci.* 82, 2022](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/soceps/v82y2022ipas0038012121000331.html)). In the thesis, cooking for all enrolled pupils had a **20–40% error**, against **2–3%** for the best forecast ([SLU](https://pub.epsilon.slu.se/22412/)). No model won in every kitchen (a neural net was best in 11 of 21).
- **Substitution acceptance** depends on the category: shoppers accept substitutes more readily where quality is ordered (margarine) than where taste decides (cereal). Personalised, context-filtered substitutes raised acceptance by 26–32% (Di Tella study, trade-press summary, **S**; [RetailWire](https://retailwire.com/discussion/substitutions-improved-online-grocery)). This fits per-kitchen mandate weights.

**Gaps to ask Valio's mentors:** online vs phone/email share, short-ship and substitution rate by segment, orders per week by segment, Aimo's share. Don't invent these on stage.

### Pitch lines (true as sourced)
1. "Finnish restaurants and food services throw away about 61 million kilos of edible food a year: 17% of all the food Finland wastes." (#1, Luke)
2. "On a serving line, about one plate in six is cooked and never eaten. Over-ordering and over-cooking for safety is the habit we target." (#2, Luke; ~16%)
3. "From 2030, EU law requires restaurants, shops and households to cut food waste per person by 30%. Kitchens will need to show their numbers." (#5)
4. "Public kitchens serve almost 380 million meals a year, and about 80% of their food is already Finnish. Origin is a rule they buy by, not a preference, so Mandate treats it as a rule." (#7, #8)
5. "Field experiments show buyers beat the algorithm where they know something it doesn't, and lose money where they don't. So Mandate decides the routine and asks the chef only at the exceptions." (Kesavan & Kushwaha 2020)
