# Idea pick

1. Read `docs/event/EVENT.md`, `docs/event/CHALLENGE.md`, `docs/event/SPONSORS.md`, `docs/strategy/WINNING.md` and `docs/archive/prep/ideas/IDEAS.md`.
2. For each candidate, write the **one-breath opener**, the **30-second wow**, the **"who would have thought" insight**, the sponsors used load-bearingly, and the **thin slice** that could be live by Sat 06:00.
3. Score with the weighted table in IDEAS.md. Show the arithmetic.
4. Pressure-test the top 2 with the `judge` skill (if installed; otherwise spawn two subagents): one judge argues each idea as the jury would (founder/VC, RL-evals lead, Context.dev CTO, Verda CTO), and one argues it's infeasible in the time left.
5. Propose a 30-minute spike per finalist that answers its riskiest question (does the API/data actually deliver the wow?).
6. Record the outcome in the IDEAS.md log and as a `D#` in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Check the rules: no relation to an existing company, nothing pre-built.
