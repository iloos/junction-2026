How it works

From a kitchen's rules to one Valio order

Mandate turns a kitchen's event into a Valio order, step by step, and at every step it orders alone only what the kitchen's mandate allows.

  1. Kitchensets the limits
  2. Eventbecomes a brief
  3. Disha menu the chef accepts
  4. Ingredientneeds, then whole packs
  5. Productknown stock first
  6. Orderone receipt

At each step, a line either goes through is held for the chefor is refused

Kitchen

The kitchen sets the limits

What the agent does

Works for one kitchen, inside its mandate: what the agent may buy alone, up to what amount, and on which days goods can arrive.

See it live: Kitchen

What it uses

  • The kitchen's mandate: caps per line, per order and per week, and a hard limit
  • Its receiving days, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday
  • The products it already buys and its origin preference

What it refuses or holds

  • HeldA delivery on a day the kitchen doesn't receive goodsoff_delivery_day · purchasing.test.ts
  • RefusedAn agent approving or changing the mandate: outside agents get no approve or mandate toolSCOPES_BY_ACTOR · adversarial.test.ts

Event

An event becomes a brief

What the agent does

Reads the request in plain words, asks for what's missing, such as dietary needs and budget, and keeps every stated requirement.

See it live: Request

What it uses

  • The event brief: occasion, guests, date and requirements
  • Gemini Flash-Lite reads the words into that brief
  • The order cut-off: 17:00 the day before delivery

What it refuses or holds

  • CaughtA date past the cut-off: the cut-off is named and the next delivery day offered, never moved silentlycheckLate · evals/check.ts
  • RefusedA product that breaks a stated requirement, such as lactose-free, even as a substituterequirement_unmet · purchasing.test.ts

Dish

The brief becomes a menu

What the agent does

Proposes dishes for the brief, with an amount per guest for each ingredient. The chef reviews the menu and accepts it before anything is planned.

See it live: Request

What it uses

  • Gemini proposes the menu, and every amount is labelled an estimate
  • The brief's guests and dietary needs, such as a vegetarian main
  • An accepted menu stays an estimate: accepting it doesn't verify a recipe

What it refuses or holds

  • HeldNothing is planned until the chef accepts the menuplan_event · core/services/events.ts

Ingredient

Each dish becomes needs, then packs

What the agent does

Turns each dish into needs in kilograms or litres, then into whole packs of what Valio sells. The need and the pack count stay apart.

See it live: Kitchen, the Why drawer

What it uses

  • Grams per guest × guests ÷ yield, each step labelled with its source
  • Valio's catalogue: 17,546 products, with pack sizes and lot steps

What it refuses or holds

  • RefusedA part pack, off Valio's lot stepBELOW_LOT · eval s3

Product

Each need gets a product from Valio's catalogue

What the agent does

Picks a product for each need, known stock first. When a product is out, it ranks substitutes and gives a reason for each one.

See it live: Kitchen, the Why drawer

What it uses

  • Valio's live price and availability for the shortlisted products, cached with the time fetched
  • Valio's own substitution rules, then name matching
  • Gemini Decisions ranks substitutes, with replay and rules fallbacks; it never approves
  • Exa web evidence in the Why drawer: net content, origin and ingredients, each cited

What it refuses or holds

  • HeldValio doesn't state a quantity: held for one tap, never counted as coverUNKNOWN_REQUIRED · validate.ts
  • HeldA product the kitchen hasn't bought beforenew_sku · tier.ts
  • HeldAn allergen change, decided apart from routine linesallergen_change · tier.ts · groups.test.ts
  • HeldA substitute that breaks the kitchen's origin preferencesubstitute_breaks_preference · validate.ts
  • HeldA product under a recall noticerecall · tier.ts
  • HeldA model's caution: a model can hold a line, never allow oneCAUTION.model_* · substitutes.ts

Order

The order passes the last checks

What the agent does

Orders what the mandate allows, holds the rest for the chef with its reason, and writes one receipt, however many times the order is sent.

Kitchen: approve and order Ops: held lines by cause Agents: connect yours

What it uses

  • The order cut-off: 17:00 the day before delivery
  • The exact terms (price, quantity, date) the chef approved
  • One locked transaction, so the same order sent twice gives one receipt
  • Honest receipts: “supplier confirmed” only where the receipt backs it; the rest read “ordered, awaiting supplier”

What it refuses or holds

  • HeldA cost over a cap, even when the order is splitCAP_EXCEEDED.weekly · tier.ts · eval s5
  • HeldAn outside agent's orderexternal_agent_needs_approval · tier.ts · eval s5
  • RefusedOver the hard limit, even split across linesover_hard_limit · ceiling.test.ts
  • RefusedAfter the ordering cut-offcutoff_passed · cutoff.test.ts · eval r5-f9
  • RefusedTerms that changed after the approval, such as a new priceTERMS_CHANGED · state.ts · eval s4

When the agent may act alone

The rules from every step, in one place. The same checks run for every caller, and a person decides everything the mandate doesn't allow.

Goes through alone

Only when every check passes:

  • Within the mandate's per-line, per-order and weekly capsCAP_EXCEEDED · tier.ts · eval s5
  • A product this kitchen already buysnew_sku · tier.ts
  • No allergen changeallergen_change · tier.ts · groups.test.ts
  • Valio states enough stockUNKNOWN_REQUIRED · validate.ts
  • A day the kitchen receives goodsoff_delivery_day · purchasing.test.ts
  • The exact terms (price, quantity, date) unchanged since the quoteTERMS_CHANGED · state.ts · eval s4

Refused

No approval passes it:

  • Over the hard limit, even split across linesover_hard_limit · ceiling.test.ts
  • After the ordering cut-offcutoff_passed · cutoff.test.ts · eval r5-f9
  • A part pack, off Valio's lot stepBELOW_LOT · eval s3
  • A product that breaks a stated requirement, such as lactose-freerequirement_unmet · purchasing.test.ts

A person approves, on the Kitchen screen, bound to the exact terms. Our agent and outside agents prepare, summarise and link the decision but never grant it: the chat refuses “approve it” and links to the approval in Kitchen, and the core refuses an approval granted by an outside agent. Any later edit voids the approval.

The three state machines

Plans, lines and approvals each move through a few fixed states. Any edit voids the approval, and an order is committed once.

Each diagram scrolls sideways.

Plan

Green is the usual path. Any edit sends the plan back to draft and clears its terms, so an earlier approval no longer applies.

Line

A line inside the mandate is ordered without asking. Any other line waits for the chef with its reasons. A line below Valio's lot step can't be ordered at all.

Approval

An approval is tied to the plan version, the exact terms, the mandate version and an expiry. If any of them changes, the order is refused.

The system mapFive zones a call passes through, and the outside services behind ports.

    Kitchen, chat, the request page, MCP and REST all call the same list of tools.

    routes/(kitchen) · server/chat · /mcp · /api/tools

    The caller comes from its key or cookie, never the request body. Outside agents get no approve scope.

    server/auth · server/tools/serve.ts

    Plain TypeScript decides: ranking, demand, mandate tiers, the terms hash.

    core/state.ts · core/rank · core/mandate

    Idempotency, a re-read of the approval, the caps and the write, in Postgres.

    server/store · pg-race.test.ts
  5. LiveEvery open screen updates

    After the commit, never before it.

    server/bus · /api/events

Design choicesSix rules that keep the agent honest, each in code you can read.
Pure core, ports at the edgesrc/lib/core imports no Svelte, Node built-ins, vendor SDKs or server code. Lint enforces it.
One function changes stateapplyOps handles 9 op types. Each op is logged with a sequence number, its caller and the door it came through.
Approval is a hashtermsHash covers the delivery date and the sorted lines. If a price changes after approval, the hash differs and the order is refused.
No lock across a network callValio, Gemini and web calls finish before the transaction starts. The lock covers only the checks and the write.
Every flow has a fixtureThe merge gate runs with LLM_PROVIDER=fixture: type check, lint, unit tests and evals through the real handlers on PGlite.
Fixed words“Supplier confirmed” is used only when a receipt backs it. Before that a line is planned, or ordered and awaiting the supplier.
