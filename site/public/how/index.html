How it works

From a kitchen's rules to one Valio order

Mandate is a purchasing agent for a kitchen. It orders from Valio on its own only what the kitchen's mandate allows, and a person decides the rest.

Every line it prepares either goes through is held for a personor is refused

The kitchen's rules

Each kitchen's own rules decide what the agent may buy

Preferences put products in order, requirements rule products out, and the mandate says what the agent may buy without asking.

Preferences

They only change the order of the list. Weights over price, reliability, Finnish origin, carbon and organic.

Kitchen.weights · core/rank/rank.ts

Requirements

A product that breaks one is out, with the reason shown.

  • Origin rules per category: prefer or require
  • Allergens the kitchen must be free from, and what to do when Valio has no record
  • Tender list only, and labels such as vegetarian on a need
Kitchen.constraints · NeedRequirements · core/mandate/categories.ts

The mandate

The chef's words and the numbers that enforce them.

  • Caps per line, per order and per week
  • A hard limit: by default the larger of five times the order cap and twice the weekly cap
  • Five decisions it never delegates: an allergen change, a new product, a broken preference, a product off the tender list, an origin requirement

Held for a person

The line waits, with its reason on screen, when it has:

  • A product the kitchen hasn't bought beforenew_sku · tier.ts
  • An allergen change, decided apart from routine linesallergen_change · groups.test.ts

Refused

No approval passes it:

  • Over the hard limit, even split across linesover_hard_limit · ceiling.test.ts
  • After the ordering cut-offcutoff_passed · cutoff.test.ts · eval r5-f9
  • A part pack, off Valio's lot stepBELOW_LOT · eval s3
  • On a request, a product that breaks a stated requirement, such as lactose-free, even as a substituterequirement_unmet · purchasing.test.ts

Only a person with a chef session approves, and only the exact terms. Our agent and outside agents prepare, summarise and link the decision. A link only finds the decision; the grant happens on the Kitchen screen or the chat's decision card. The core refuses an approval recorded by an outside agent, and any later edit voids the approval.

The agent

The agent prepares the order and leaves the approval to the chef

It reads what the kitchen asks for, works in a few bounded steps, and stops at a decision the chef must make.

  • Six kinds of ask, sorted by rules before any modelA routine order, the seeded event, a shortage repair, a change to a need, a question about a plan, or something it doesn't do.server/agent/intent.ts
  • At most 8 stepsEach run has a step limit, a deadline and a cancel button.server/agent/loop.ts · runs.ts
  • A scripted planner on the live appThe steps follow a fixed script. A Gemini Flash-Lite planner exists as an opt-in and is off on the live app.AGENT_PLANNER=llm · server/adapters/llm/router.ts
  • Typed facts about each productThe planner reads short, typed fields. A supplier's product text is data and can't give it instructions.server/agent/facts.test.ts · adversarial.test.ts
  • One clarifying questionWhen something it needs is missing, such as the day, it asks once and goes on. Its reply uses only numbers from the task's ledger.server/chat/{question,explain}.ts
  • It never approves“Approve it” in chat is refused, with a pointer to the decision for the chef.server/chat/understand.ts · evals/chat · dock-approve.e2e.ts

Limits today: in chat the agent plans only the seeded buffet, so a new event goes through the Request page; and a second item in the same message is dropped.

Five flows

Five ways an order moves through Mandate

Each flow is a short list of steps with the code that runs it, and a case recorded from the live app where we have one.

One item

A request in plain words becomes one checked line, ordered alone or held for the chef.

  1. The words come in on the Request page or in chat, such as “10 l lactose-free milk for Friday”.server/request/run.ts · /api/request
  2. Sorted as a purchase, an event, or a dish with a count.server/request/classify.ts
  3. Read by rules first, then Gemini Flash-Lite. The reading is checked so no stated word is lost.server/request/{parse,reading}.ts
  4. The date is checked against the cut-off. A late date is named, and the next delivery day is offered.server/request/dates.ts · evals/check.ts
  5. A catalogue search makes a shortlist of up to 5 with reasons, drops products that contradict the request, and asks Valio live for the shortlist's prices and stock.server/request/{lookup,checks}.ts · core/services/quote.ts
  6. The amount becomes packs by printed content. Conflicting content holds the line.server/request/convert.ts · core/demand/convert.ts
  7. A size check against the kitchen's own numbers.server/request/sanity.ts
  8. The mandate decides: ordered alone, or held for approval.core/services/orders.ts · core/mandate/tier.ts

Try it: Request

Event to menu

An event brief becomes a menu the chef accepts, then needs, then whole packs.

  1. A brief in words, such as a Christmas dinner for 50, and questions one at a time as chips: guests, date, diet, budget. Rules read the brief's guests, date and requirements, with no model call; Gemini Flash-Lite reads product requests.server/menu/brief.ts
  2. Gemini 3.8 Flash proposes a menu. Every amount is labelled an estimate; without the model, the team's dish templates stand in.server/menu/{proposal,rubric}.ts
  3. The chef edits amounts and accepts the menu. Nothing is planned before that.server/menu/flow.ts · MenuReview.svelte
  4. Each ingredient becomes a need: guests × amount per guest ÷ yield, with no model.core/demand/menu.ts · core/services/events.ts
  5. Each need is quoted and checked against the mandate; a product that's out goes to the out-of-stock flow.core/services/{quote,substitutes}.ts
  6. Changing the grams per guest recomputes the line in the same call and voids any approval.core/services/events.ts · edit_line
  7. One decision per reason group, then the order and its receipt.ApprovalPanel.svelte · core/services/orders.ts

We don't yet claim the right product for every menu dish: each line is shown to the chef for review.

Out of stock

When Valio is out, the agent finds and sizes substitutes, ranks them by the kitchen's rules, and holds anything new for the chef.

  1. A line is quoted out of stock, undeliverable or unknown, each in its own words.core/services/wording.ts
  2. Candidates come from Valio's own substitution rules, then the scenario's hand-checked rescues, then same-category products found by name.core/services/substitutes.ts · catalog/retrieval.ts
  3. Each candidate is sized to the same need.core/demand/sizing.ts
  4. Requirements rule candidates out, each with its reason. Known stock ranks first, then the kitchen's preferences. Gemini Decisions ranks substitutes as one input to the kitchen's ranking; it never approves.core/rank/rank.ts · server/adapters/decide
  5. Making it in house is an option when every ingredient can be ordered.core/services/substitutes.ts · D8a
  6. A new product, an allergen change or a broken preference holds the line for the chef.RescueCompare.svelte · core/mandate/tier.ts
  7. If another kitchen takes the stock, the rest is sized to the next candidate and the approval is voided. Tested, simulated.core/services/contention.ts
  8. Still nothing: options outside the catalogue, for information only. They are never ordered.server/chat/outside.ts · server/web/lookup.ts

Outside agent

Your own agent connects over MCP or REST, uses the same tools through the same checks, and every line it orders waits for the chef.

  1. On the Agents page each visitor gets their own key and MCP address, working only in their own sandbox.routes/agents · /api/demo/judge-key
  2. The agent reads its context first: who it is, what it may do, the kitchens, mandates and open plans.server/agent/get-context.ts
  3. It searches, quotes, then places an order or plans an event. An unknown product is refused before any call to Valio.server/tools/{serve,handlers}.ts
  4. Its lines are always held. The result carries a summary, the next action and a link to the decision.server/tools/present.ts · core/mandate/tier.ts
  5. If it tries to approve or change the mandate, it gets 403 and Ops logs the refusal.server/tools/refusals.ts
  6. The chef approves in the Kitchen, and the agent reads the receipt.server/tasks/outcome.ts

Each key is metered: 120 calls, 30 writes and 20 quotes a minute.server/tools/key-limits.ts

Approval

The chef approves exact terms. Any edit voids the approval, and the order is checked again before it's placed.

  1. Held lines are grouped by reason, and the chef decides one group at a time, so an allergen change never rides with routine lines.core/services/orders.ts · groups.test.ts
  2. Press and hold to confirm the exact terms, in the Kitchen or on the chat's decision card.HoldButton.svelte · DecisionCard.svelte
  3. The approval is bound to the plan version, the terms, the mandate version and an expiry.core/hash.ts · core/state.ts
  4. Any edit or substitute sends the plan back to draft and voids the approval, and the plan shows what changed. Tested; in the app's screens, not yet checked on the live app.core/state.ts · eval r6-5
  5. The order re-checks everything under a lock: version, terms, mandate, expiry, cut-off, caps. Any mismatch is refused.core/services/orders.ts · eval s4
  6. One receipt, however many times the order is sent.evals s1, s5 · pg-race.test.ts · smoke-race

The three state machines

Each diagram scrolls sideways.

Plan

Green is the usual path. Any edit sends the plan back to draft and clears its terms, so an earlier approval no longer applies.

Line

A line inside the mandate is ordered without asking. Any other line waits for the chef with its reasons. A line below Valio's lot step can't be ordered at all.

Approval

An approval is tied to the plan version, the exact terms, the mandate version and an expiry. If any of them changes, the order is refused.
