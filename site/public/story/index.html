    Five supply states, shown on every amount.

    Solid, in two colours: Valio stated it, or the supplier confirmed it. Hatched: Valio states no quantity, or the supplier hasn't confirmed yet. Dashed: nothing covers it.

    Supplier confirmedbacked by a receipt
    Ordered, awaiting suppliercommitted, the supplier has not confirmed yet
